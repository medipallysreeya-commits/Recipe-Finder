RECIPE: VEGETABLE FRIED RICE

INGREDIENTS:
Rice, carrot, peas, beans, onion, garlic, soy sauce, pepper, oil, salt.

INSTRUCTIONS:
Cook the rice and allow it to cool.
Heat oil in a large pan.
Add garlic and onion.
Add carrot, peas and beans.
Add cooked rice and soy sauce.
Season with pepper and salt.
Mix well and serve hot.


RECIPE: LEMON RICE

INGREDIENTS:
Rice, lemon, peanuts, green chilli, curry leaves, mustard seeds, turmeric, oil, salt.

INSTRUCTIONS:
Cook the rice and keep it aside.
Heat oil and add mustard seeds.
Add peanuts, green chilli and curry leaves.
Add turmeric and salt.
Add cooked rice and mix well.
Turn off the heat and add lemon juice.
Serve warm.


RECIPE: CURD RICE

INGREDIENTS:
Rice, curd, milk, ginger, green chilli, curry leaves, mustard seeds, oil, salt.

INSTRUCTIONS:
Cook the rice until soft.
Allow the rice to cool.
Mix rice with curd and milk.
Heat oil and add mustard seeds.
Add ginger, green chilli and curry leaves.
Pour the tempering over the rice.
Mix well and serve.


RECIPE: MUSHROOM RICE

INGREDIENTS:
Rice, mushroom, onion, tomato, garlic, ginger, pepper, turmeric, oil, salt.

INSTRUCTIONS:
Cook the rice and keep it aside.
Heat oil and add onion.
Add garlic and ginger.
Add chopped mushroom and tomato.
Add turmeric, pepper and salt.
Add cooked rice and mix well.
Cook for a few minutes and serve.


RECIPE: JEERA RICE

INGREDIENTS:
Basmati rice, cumin seeds, butter, onion, green chilli, coriander, salt.

INSTRUCTIONS:
Wash and soak the basmati rice.
Heat butter and add cumin seeds.
Add onion and green chilli.
Add rice and water.
Cook until the rice is tender.
Garnish with coriander and serve hot.
