RECIPE: TOMATO RICE

INGREDIENTS:
Rice, tomato, onion, green chilli, ginger, garlic, oil, salt, turmeric, red chilli powder, garam masala.

INSTRUCTIONS:
Wash the rice and cook it until soft.
Heat oil in a pan and add onion.
Add ginger, garlic and green chilli.
Add chopped tomato and cook until soft.
Add turmeric, salt, red chilli powder and garam masala.
Add the cooked rice and mix everything well.
Cook for a few minutes and serve hot.


RECIPE: PANEER BUTTER MASALA

INGREDIENTS:
Paneer, tomato, onion, butter, cream, ginger, garlic, turmeric, red chilli powder, garam masala, salt.

INSTRUCTIONS:
Cut the paneer into small cubes.
Heat butter and add onion.
Add ginger and garlic and cook well.
Add tomato and spices.
Add cream and cook the gravy.
Add paneer and simmer for a few minutes.
Serve hot with rice or naan.


RECIPE: CHANA MASALA

INGREDIENTS:
Chickpeas, onion, tomato, ginger, garlic, green chilli, oil, cumin, turmeric, coriander powder, salt.

INSTRUCTIONS:
Soak chickpeas overnight and boil them.
Heat oil and add cumin.
Add onion, ginger and garlic.
Add tomato and spices.
Add the boiled chickpeas.
Cook until the gravy becomes thick.
Serve hot with rice or roti.


RECIPE: VEGETABLE BIRYANI

INGREDIENTS:
Basmati rice, carrot, peas, potato, beans, onion, tomato, ginger, garlic, yoghurt, mint, spices, oil, salt.

INSTRUCTIONS:
Wash and soak the basmati rice.
Heat oil and fry onion until golden.
Add ginger, garlic, tomato and vegetables.
Add yoghurt and spices.
Add soaked rice and water.
Cook until the rice is tender.
Garnish with mint and serve hot.


RECIPE: ALOO GOBI

INGREDIENTS:
Potato, cauliflower, tomato, onion, ginger, garlic, turmeric, cumin, coriander powder, oil, salt.

INSTRUCTIONS:
Cut the potato and cauliflower into pieces.
Heat oil and add cumin.
Add onion, ginger and garlic.
Add tomato and spices.
Add potato and cauliflower.
Cover and cook until the vegetables are tender.
Serve hot with roti.
