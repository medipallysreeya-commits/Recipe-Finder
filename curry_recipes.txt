RECIPE: POTATO CURRY

INGREDIENTS:
Potato, onion, tomato, ginger, garlic, turmeric, cumin, coriander powder, chilli powder, oil, salt.

INSTRUCTIONS:
Cut potatoes into small pieces.
Heat oil and add cumin.
Add onion, ginger and garlic.
Add tomato and spices.
Add potato and mix well.
Add water and cook until the potato is tender.
Serve hot with rice or roti.


RECIPE: MIXED VEGETABLE CURRY

INGREDIENTS:
Potato, carrot, peas, beans, cauliflower, tomato, onion, ginger, garlic, turmeric, garam masala, oil, salt.

INSTRUCTIONS:
Cut all vegetables into small pieces.
Heat oil and fry onion.
Add ginger, garlic and tomato.
Add turmeric and garam masala.
Add all vegetables and water.
Cover and cook until tender.
Serve hot with rice or roti.


RECIPE: PALAK PANEER

INGREDIENTS:
Spinach, paneer, onion, tomato, ginger, garlic, cream, cumin, turmeric, garam masala, oil, salt.

INSTRUCTIONS:
Blanch the spinach and make a smooth paste.
Heat oil and add cumin.
Add onion, ginger and garlic.
Add tomato and cook well.
Add spinach paste and spices.
Add paneer and cream.
Cook for a few minutes and serve.


RECIPE: DAL TADKA

INGREDIENTS:
Toor dal, onion, tomato, garlic, ginger, cumin, turmeric, chilli powder, coriander, oil, salt.

INSTRUCTIONS:
Wash and pressure cook the dal.
Heat oil in a pan.
Add cumin, garlic and ginger.
Add onion and tomato.
Add turmeric and chilli powder.
Add cooked dal and water.
Simmer and garnish with coriander.


RECIPE: RAJMA MASALA

INGREDIENTS:
Rajma, onion, tomato, ginger, garlic, cumin, turmeric, chilli powder, garam masala, oil, salt.

INSTRUCTIONS:
Soak rajma overnight.
Pressure cook the rajma until soft.
Heat oil and add cumin.
Add onion, ginger, garlic and tomato.
Add spices and cook the gravy.
Add rajma and simmer.
Serve hot with rice.
