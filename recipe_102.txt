RECIPE: Dal Tadka

CATEGORY: Vegetarian
KEYWORDS: Toor Dal, Tomato, Onion, Garlic, Cumin

INGREDIENTS:
- Toor Dal
- Tomato
- Onion
- Garlic
- Cumin

INSTRUCTIONS:
