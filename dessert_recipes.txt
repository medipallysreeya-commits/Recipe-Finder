RECIPE: RICE KHEER

INGREDIENTS:
Rice, milk, sugar, cardamom, almonds, cashews, raisins.

INSTRUCTIONS:
Wash the rice and soak it briefly.
Boil milk in a heavy pan.
Add rice and cook slowly.
Add sugar and cardamom.
Add almonds, cashews and raisins.
Cook until the kheer becomes creamy.
Serve warm or chilled.


RECIPE: GAJAR HALWA

INGREDIENTS:
Carrot, milk, sugar, ghee, cardamom, almonds, cashews, raisins.

INSTRUCTIONS:
Grate the carrots.
Heat ghee and add carrots.
Cook until the carrots become soft.
Add milk and cook slowly.
Add sugar and cardamom.
Garnish with almonds, cashews and raisins.


RECIPE: BANANA PANCAKE

INGREDIENTS:
Banana, flour, milk, sugar, baking powder, butter, cinnamon.

INSTRUCTIONS:
Mash the banana in a bowl.
Add flour, milk, sugar and baking powder.
Mix into a smooth batter.
Heat butter in a pan.
Pour the batter and cook both sides.
Add cinnamon and serve warm.


RECIPE: FRUIT CUSTARD

INGREDIENTS:
Milk, custard powder, sugar, apple, banana, grapes, mango.

INSTRUCTIONS:
Boil the milk.
Mix custard powder with a little cold milk.
Add the mixture to the hot milk.
Add sugar and cook until thick.
Allow the custard to cool.
Add chopped fruits and refrigerate.
Serve chilled.


RECIPE: SEMOLINA HALWA

INGREDIENTS:
Semolina, sugar, ghee, milk, cardamom, almonds, cashews, raisins.

INSTRUCTIONS:
Heat ghee in a pan.
Add semolina and roast until golden.
Add hot milk carefully.
Add sugar and cardamom.
Cook until thick.
Garnish with almonds, cashews and raisins.
Serve warm.
