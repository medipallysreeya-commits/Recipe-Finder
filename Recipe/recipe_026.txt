RECIPE: Palak Paneer 3

CATEGORY: Vegetarian
KEYWORDS: Spinach, Paneer, Onion, Tomato, Garlic, Garam Masala

INGREDIENTS:
- Spinach
- Paneer
- Onion
- Tomato
- Garlic
- Garam Masala

INSTRUCTIONS:
Blanch spinach and blend. Cook with onion and tomato, then add paneer and spices.
