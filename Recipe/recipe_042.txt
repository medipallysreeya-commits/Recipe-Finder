RECIPE: Vegetable Pulao 5

CATEGORY: Vegetarian
KEYWORDS: Basmati Rice, Carrot, Beans, Peas, Onion, Garam Masala

INGREDIENTS:
- Basmati Rice
- Carrot
- Beans
- Peas
- Onion
- Garam Masala

INSTRUCTIONS:
Saute onion and vegetables. Add rice, water and spices, then cook until tender.
