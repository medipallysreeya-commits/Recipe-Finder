RECIPE: VEGETABLE UPMA

INGREDIENTS:
Semolina, onion, carrot, peas, green chilli, ginger, mustard seeds, curry leaves, oil, salt.

INSTRUCTIONS:
Roast the semolina until lightly golden.
Heat oil and add mustard seeds.
Add curry leaves, onion, ginger and green chilli.
Add carrot and peas.
Add water and salt.
Slowly add roasted semolina while stirring.
Cook until soft and serve hot.


RECIPE: MASALA DOSA

INGREDIENTS:
Rice, urad dal, potato, onion, green chilli, curry leaves, mustard seeds, turmeric, oil, salt.

INSTRUCTIONS:
Soak rice and urad dal separately.
Grind them into a smooth batter.
Ferment the batter overnight.
Boil and mash the potato.
Prepare potato masala with onion and spices.
Spread the dosa batter on a hot pan.
Add potato masala and fold the dosa.
Serve hot with chutney.


RECIPE: POHA

INGREDIENTS:
Poha, onion, potato, peanuts, green chilli, mustard seeds, turmeric, lemon, coriander, oil, salt.

INSTRUCTIONS:
Wash the poha and drain the water.
Heat oil and add mustard seeds.
Add peanuts, onion and green chilli.
Add potato and turmeric.
Add poha and salt.
Cook for a few minutes.
Add lemon juice and coriander before serving.


RECIPE: VEGETABLE SANDWICH

INGREDIENTS:
Bread, potato, tomato, onion, cucumber, carrot, butter, pepper, salt.

INSTRUCTIONS:
Boil and mash the potato.
Slice tomato, onion and cucumber.
Spread butter on the bread.
Add vegetables and mashed potato.
Season with pepper and salt.
Toast the sandwich until golden.


RECIPE: OATS PORRIDGE

INGREDIENTS:
Oats, milk, banana, apple, honey, nuts, cinnamon.

INSTRUCTIONS:
Add oats and milk to a pan.
Cook on medium heat until soft.
Add banana and apple.
Add honey and cinnamon.
Garnish with nuts and serve warm.
